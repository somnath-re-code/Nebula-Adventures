Free stargazing trips at high altitude

Thinner air and less city light mean far more stars. We guide small groups to high, dark places where the Milky Way is easy to see.

Who can come

Students and anyone interested in astronomy. No experience needed.

What to bring

Warm layers, a water bottle and a torch with a red light. We provide telescopes and guides.

How a night works

We travel up together, let our eyes adjust to the dark, then explore the sky with a guide until late.

Frequently asked questions

Are the stargazing trips really free?

Yes. Every trip is free for students and anyone interested in astronomy.

Do I need a telescope or experience?

No. We bring telescopes and guides, and our courses start from zero.

Why do you go to high altitudes?

Higher places have thinner air and less light pollution, so many more stars become visible.
